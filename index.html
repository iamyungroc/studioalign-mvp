The House Building Rule

Making a record is like building a house. We pour the foundation (drums/bass) before we paint the walls (synths/guitars). Revisions on arrangement must happen before we mix.

Vocal and Mental Prep

  • Hydrate: Room-temperature water for 24 hours prior. No ice water or dairy.
  • Rest: Vocal cords need sleep. No late nights before recording.
  • Know Your Lyrics: We are here to capture emotion, not read off a screen.

The Feedback Agreement

To get your song to radio-ready standards, we use the AllEars Mix Feedback tab for all revisions. No random text messages or voice notes. This ensures every change is tracked and executed perfectly.
