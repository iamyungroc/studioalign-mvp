The Studio Protocol

To ensure radio-ready results, we adhere to strict professional standards.

The House Building Rule

We pour the foundation (drums/bass) before painting walls (synths). Arrangement changes must happen before mixing.

Vocal Readiness

  • Hydrate: Room-temp water 24hrs prior. No dairy.
  • Rest: Vocal cords need sleep.
  • Lyrics: Memorized. We capture emotion, not reading.

The Feedback Agreement

All revisions go through the AllEars Mix Feedback system. No random text messages or voice notes.

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: September 2026

By using the AllEars platform and booking a session with RocAudio Tech Company, you agree to the following terms:

1. Booking & Payment

  • All sessions must be booked through the AllEars platform.
  • Payment is required to confirm your booking. Sessions are not confirmed until payment is received.
  • Prices are quoted in Nigerian Naira (NGN) and are non-negotiable once confirmed.

2. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session are non-refundable.
  • Rescheduling is allowed once, with at least 24 hours notice, at no extra cost.
  • No-shows forfeit the full session fee.

3. Revisions

  • Each project includes up to 2 free revisions after the initial mix delivery.
  • Additional revisions beyond the included limit will be charged at a per-revision rate, quoted before work begins.
  • All revision notes must be submitted through the AllEars Mix Feedback system. Notes sent via text, WhatsApp, or voice notes may be disregarded or delayed.

4. File Delivery

  • Final deliverables (mix, master, stems) will be delivered within the agreed timeline.
  • Files will be delivered as high-quality WAV files unless otherwise agreed.
  • Project files (DAW sessions) are not included in the deliverables unless separately purchased.

5. Intellectual Property

  • The artist retains full ownership of their composition, lyrics, and master recording upon full payment.
  • RocAudio reserves the right to use short audio clips (under 30 seconds) for portfolio and promotional purposes, unless the artist requests otherwise in writing.

6. Studio Conduct

  • Artists must arrive prepared as outlined in the Studio Protocol (Step 5).
  • Excessive lateness (over 30 minutes) may result in a shortened session with no partial refund.
  • RocAudio reserves the right to end a session if the artist is under the influence of substances that impair the recording process.

7. Liability

  • RocAudio is not responsible for data loss due to hardware failure, power outages, or unforeseen technical issues. All project files are backed up regularly.
